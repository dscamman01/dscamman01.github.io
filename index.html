The house

3 bedrooms3 bathsSleeps 8

The main floor is one open space: kitchen, dining, and living area together, plus a bedroom with two twin beds next to a full bath.

Upstairs, there's an ensuite bedroom with two queen beds and a big TV, and the primary suite, with a sitting area, a large bath, and an oversized closet.

Out back, the private deck has a hot tub and BBQ. The Eaglewood seasonal pool is right next door, and you're close to the Woodlands Golf Course and Sunriver's bike paths.
